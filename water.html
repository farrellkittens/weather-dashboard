On-Water Checklist

PFD: Colorado requires an appropriately sized life jacket readily accessible for each person on a vessel; wearing it is the safer choice.
Cold water: Mountain lakes and runoff-fed rivers can be dangerously cold even on warm days.
River flows: Tubing suitability changes quickly. Avoid high water, low-head dams, strainers, and sections beyond your ability.
Clean gear: Clean, drain, and dry paddleboards, tubes, paddles, and other equipment between waters.
